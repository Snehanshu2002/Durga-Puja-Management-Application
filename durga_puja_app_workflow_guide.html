🌺 Durga Puja Management App

Aam Bhasha me Samajhiye: Chanda lene se lekar Kharcha, Approval aur Balance Sheet tak ka Pura Process

Non-IT Committee Guide

🎯 1. Is App Ka Asli Maqsad Kya Hai?

Durga Puja, Bhasani Utsav ya Natak (Opera/Jatra) jaise bade events me har saal lakho rupaye ka len-den hota hai. Pehle kacchi paper slips aur diary me hisab likhne se paise ka hisab gadbad ho jata tha aur vivad hote the.

Yeh App in sabhi samasyaon ko 100% digital aur transparent banata hai. Har ek rupaye ki raseed digital banti hai aur bina double-check approval ke koi entry final balance sheet me nahi jati.

👥 2. Kisko Kya Zimmedari Di Gayi Hai? (6 Main Roles)

Role (Padvi) Asli Zindagi me Kaun Hai? Kya Kaam Kar Sakta Hai? Kya Nahi Kar Sakta?
1. Super Admin Samiti Mukhiya / IT In-charge Naye Events banana, Naye Users jodna, Categories banana. Approved hisab delete/edit nahi kar sakta.
2. President (Sabhapati) Committee President / Adhyaksh Pura live dashboard dekhna, Chanda/Kharcha approve karna. Users add/deactivate nahi kar sakta.
3. Treasurer (Khazanchi) Samiti ka Cashier / Khazanchi Cash sambhalna, Kharcha approve karna, Bank me jama karna. Apni banayi entry khud approve nahi kar sakta.
4. Manager Pandal / Event Manager Daily kharche ki entry dalna, Chanda target dekhna. Dusro ke records approve nahi kar sakta.
5. Collector Chanda lene wale Sadasya Digital raseed banana, cash Treasurer ko handover karna. Koi entry approve nahi kar sakta.
6. Viewer Senior Trustees / Auditor Sirf hisab aur balance sheet dekh sakta hai (Read-Only). Koi bhi data change/add/delete nahi kar sakta.

🔄 3. Step-by-Step Pura Process (Workflow)

🔹 STEP 1: Event & Targets Set Karna (Budgeting)

• Super Admin naya event banata hai (Jaise: Bhasani Utsab 2026 ya Opera Jatra 2026).

• Har ek sadasya ka Chanda Target set hota hai taaki live pata chale ki kiska kitna target baki hai.

🔹 STEP 2: Chanda Lene Ka Process (Collection Flow)

• Collector donor ke paas jata hai aur app me "Add Collection" karta hai.

• Payment mode: Cash, UPI (GPay/PhonePe), Bank Transfer ya Cheque.

• Donor ke mobile par turant digital receipt ban jati hai.

• Status rehta hai: PENDING (Abhi final hisab me nahi juda).

🔹 STEP 3: 4-Eyes Approval Rule (Double Check Suraksha)

• Treasurer ya President entry ko check karke "APPROVE" karte hain.

• Suraksha Niyam: Jisne entry dali hai wo khud use approve nahi kar sakta (Self-Approval Blocked).

• Approve hote hi official Receipt No. lag jata hai aur final hisab me jud jata hai.

🔹 STEP 4: Cash Custody / Hath-badli (Cash Handover)

• Din ke end me Collector jitna cash ikathha karta hai (Jaise ₹10,000), wo Treasurer ko physical de deta hai.

• Collector app me "Handover Cash" request karta hai, aur Treasurer "Accept" dabata hai.

• Isse Collector ki liability khatam ho jati hai aur cash Treasurer ke naam pe shift ho jata hai.

🔹 STEP 5: Kharcha (Expense Management)

• Pandal, Light, Sound, Idol ya Bhog kharche ka bill photo ke sath app me upload hota hai.

• Treasurer / President bill dekhkar approve karte hain tabhi kharcha pass hota hai.

🔹 STEP 6: Bank Deposit & Final Balance Sheet

• Treasurer ikathha hua cash samiti ke official Bank account me jama karke challan number update karta hai.

• Meeting me ek click par PDF / Excel Report nikal aati hai.

🔒 4. Suraksha Niyam (Kyun Isme Koi Ghapla Nahi Ho Sakta?)

1. Approved Records Locked: Ek baar entry approve ho gayi toh use koi bhi delete ya modify nahi kar sakta.
2. Single-Device Security: Ek user ek phone me login hai to dusre phone me open karte hi pehla logout ho jayega.
3. Audit Log: Har ek click, approval aur deletion ka digital record date aur time ke sath rehta hai.

📢 5. Samiti Meeting me 1 Minute me Bolne ke Points:

  • 1. "Ghar-ghar chanda lena 100% digital aur transparent ho gaya hai — donor ko digital receipt milti hai."
  • 2. "Collector jab tak Treasurer ko cash dekar confirm nahi karwayega, tab tak paisa Collector ke hisab me dikhega."
  • 3. "Koi bhi kharcha bina bill photo aur adhyaksh/treasurer approval ke pass nahi ho sakta."
  • 4. "Utsav khatam hone ke baad kisi ko hisab jodne ki zaroorat nahi — ek click me puri Balance Sheet PDF me print ho jati hai."
